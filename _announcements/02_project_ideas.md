### Note on proposing your own project

If you are proposing your own project, please send an e-mail to both myself and Iman.  The e-mail should contain a short description of your proposed project, including the title, what you propose to do, and why you think it is directly related to the type of material we are covering in this course (i.e. algorithmic genomics).  We will likely approve proposals unless they are substantially out of scope (or too large/small), and will attempt to respond with the approvals within a couple of days.
