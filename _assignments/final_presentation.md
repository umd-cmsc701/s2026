# Final project lightning presentation 

The Google Sheet for final project presentation signup is located [here](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1XAIlRZ5s3dfKYulgHWw2ADyTQnJdgrFB9FNTAn1UiwQ/edit?usp=sharing). **Note**: You must use your `umd.edu` domain account to enter your group in the spreadsheet and the signup is first come/first serve . The presentations will take place next week (May 5 and May 7). Because of the number of groups, we are on a *tight* schedule.  As such, here are some notes / suggestions.

 - You should plan for your presentation to take **5 minutes**.
 - You should upload your slides (either as a Google Sides presentation, or as a PDF to [*this* shared folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1Mv3uw8ZHRJOmbJAA9q3BZNkG9Zcko2pX?usp=sharing)), _before_ the class in which your group is to present. Like the presentation signup sheet, you must use your `umd.edu` domain account to upload your slides.


Finally, some notes on the presentation itself and expectations. **It is not expected** that you present final results from your project in your presentation. We realize that many of you still have experiments to perform, results to analyze, and implementations to finish by the final report deadline.  Thus, it is OK if you don't have final results to present in your talk. The presentation will be evaluated based on the following points:

  - Problem formulation (what is the problem your group is tackling)
  - Problem motivation (why is this an important, useful, and computationally (and/or biologically) interesting problem)
  - Current state-of-the-art / solutions (what is your baseline? why might it be possible to improve the state-of-the-art)
  - Your approach
    - What is it that your group is doing?
    - What is interesting, novel, or promising about it?
    - *If you have them*: Any preliminary results or experiments
    - What remains for the final project report?
